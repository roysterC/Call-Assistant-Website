## Rhythm
Three feed posts a week, about an hour a week to schedule and reply. Posting times are a starting guess: **Monday, Wednesday and Sunday around 8pm**, when owners are off the floor (Monday is a common closing day; Saturday is the busiest day, so skip it). Move them once Instagram's insights show when your followers are online.

The day after each feed post, share it to Stories with a link sticker to the booking page.

## Engagement (15 minutes, the day after each post)
- Reply to every comment and DM the same day, as Kikai.
- Follow UK salon owners, salon suppliers and hairdressing educators. Like and leave a useful comment when there's something real to say. Never paste a pitch into someone's comments.
- A DM that asks about price or setup gets the booking link and a short, specific answer.

## What to track
Weekly, per post: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link taps and DMs. Monthly: booked calls that came from Instagram or Facebook (ask "how did you hear about us?" on every call).

Starting hypotheses to check after four weeks:
1. Real-screen posts (4, 8, 11) get more saves than scene posts (1, 2, 6); scene posts get more reach.
2. The two question posts (7, 9) bring more DMs than any other pillar.

Record what you find in the Learnings table of `.agents/product-marketing.md`.
